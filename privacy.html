Privacy Policy for Pack the Sleigh

Publisher: AndMore Games
App: Pack the Sleigh (iPhone and iPad, free with ads)
Effective date: November 24, 2026
Contact: andmore.games.help@gmail.com

Pack the Sleigh is a Christmas puzzle game. The game itself collects no account, no name, no email, no location, and no payment information from you. Your progress — candy canes, sack and elf upgrades, reindeer team, unlocked cities and best scores — is stored only on your device.

Advertising — Google AdMob

Pack the Sleigh is free and supported by ads shown before each city. We use Google AdMob (Google LLC) to show those ads. AdMob app ID: ca-app-pub-1206009378507428~5247592169. AdMob and its partners may collect and use: your device's advertising identifier, advertising data, information about how you interact with the app and with ads (such as app launches, taps and video views), crash and performance data, and your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location. This data is used to serve ads, including personalised ads where permitted, and to measure ads. Google's use of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.” Google provides the same or equal protection for the data it receives as described in this policy.

Tracking permission (iPhone / iPad)

On iOS, AdMob can use your device's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking for Pack the Sleigh. If tracking is not allowed, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalised. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Children

Pack the Sleigh is rated 4+. It is not directed at collecting personal information from children, and we do not knowingly collect any.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no account data about you, so there is nothing on our side to delete. AdMob retains the advertising data it collects under Google's own retention rules. To reset the advertising identifier on your device, use iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or reset it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here and the effective date above will be updated.
