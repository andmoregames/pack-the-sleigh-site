Stuck on a city, lost your progress, or found a bug? Email us — it goes straight to the developer.

Email us for help

Or email us directly at andmore.games.help@gmail.com. The button opens your mail app with the subject already filled in.

When you write, tell us: your device (iPhone / iPad), iOS version, which city it happened on, and what you saw. A screenshot helps.

Common answers

  • Progress is saved on your device, per city. Deleting the app deletes your saved candy canes, sack and elf upgrades, reindeer team and unlocked cities.
  • Picks are final once a present is loaded into a bag — tap carefully.
  • Ads appear before each city, never in the middle of a city.
  • Pack the Sleigh is free. There are no in-app purchases.
